# Controlled Discomfort
**Status:** `[PRE-FORMAL]` Concept / Published  
**Category:** Self-Leadership / Capacity Building

## Definition
The deliberate exposure to manageable, high-intensity environments to train nervous system regulation, expand baseline tolerance, and prove operational capacity before real-world pressure arrives.

## Core Language (From Text)
- "Choose one quality you want to build this week, and create a safe but stretching experience that trains your system on purpose."
- "Resilience is built through adaptation."
- "Discomfort is not always a sign that something is wrong. Sometimes, it means life is asking you to become steadier."

## Function in Narrative
Establishes the practical training methodology for resilience. Moves beyond theory into applied physiological and psychological conditioning.

## Evolution Note
Published as a training philosophy. Later formalized as a core application layer within the INNERstanding Calibration Cycle, specifically tied to Pattern Interruption and Recalibration.
