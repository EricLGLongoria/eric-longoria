# Inner Notifications
**Status:** Public / Formalized  
**Category:** Self-Leadership / Signal Detection

## Definition
Internal signals (body, emotion, thought, intuition) that appear before conscious reasoning completes its explanation. They are the system's early warning data.

## Core Language
- "What notification have you been swiping away?"
- "Inner notifications are internal signals that ask for attention."

## Function
Serves as the primary signal-detection layer. Teaches clients to observe rather than dismiss or overreact to early system data.
