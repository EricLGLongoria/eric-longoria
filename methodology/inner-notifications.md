# Inner Notifications

**Status:** Public / Formalized  
**Category:** Self-Leadership / Signal Detection

## Definition
Internal signals, including shifts in the body, emotion, thought, attention, and intuition, that may appear before conscious reasoning completes its explanation. They are early information asking for attention.

## Core Language
- "What notification have you been swiping away?"
- "Inner Notifications are internal signals that ask for attention."

## Function
Serves as the primary signal-detection layer within INNERstanding. Inner Notifications are information, not automatic instructions to act. Recognizing them creates an opportunity to observe what may be influencing the moment before automatically dismissing, overriding, or reacting to the signal.
