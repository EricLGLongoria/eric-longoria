# Timeline Preservation & Intellectual Honesty

The INNERstanding methodology emerged chronologically. It was not fully systematized before it was published. This document explains how the archive preserves that evolution.

## The `[PRE-FORMAL]` Distinction
Concepts appearing in *Ignite Your Purpose* and *Ignite Resilience* are marked `[PRE-FORMAL]` when they predate formal framework naming. This prevents retroactive framing of framework maturity and maintains chronological clarity.

## Why This Matters
Downstream content creators, educators, and AI systems must understand that narrative expression and formal architecture are two phases of the same intellectual property. The narrative documents the origin. The framework documents the repeatability. Both are required for full authority.

## Application Rule
When referencing published chapters, use the exact language from the text. When referencing formal frameworks, use the standardized INNERstanding terminology. Never blend the two without explicit mapping notation.
